Land in Judea & Samaria

Land identification, verification of rights and documents, registration, regularization and acquisition.

Thorough Review Throughout the Process

Our activity in Judea & Samaria combines identifying land and rights holders, reviewing documents and planning status, completing required documentation, and supporting registration and regularization processes through the evaluation of a possible acquisition.

Areas of Activity

Land IdentificationOwners & DocumentsStatus ReviewPlanning & RisksRegistration & RegularizationLand Acquisition
01

Land Identification

Identifying land based on Malia documents, Jordanian or Palestinian land registry extracts and other relevant information.

02

Identifying Owners and Completing Documents

Identifying rights holders and reviewing existing documents, together with completing authentic documentation required for the next stages of the process.

03

Land Status Review

Reviewing the status of the land and the rights in it according to the available documents and information relating to the property.

04

Planning Status and Potential

Evaluating the current planning status, planning potential and risks associated with the land’s location and the registration process.

05

Registration and Regularization

Advancing land registration and regularization procedures according to the characteristics of the land, the rights and the relevant documents.

06

Land Acquisition

Evaluating opportunities and acquiring land suitable for the company’s activities.
