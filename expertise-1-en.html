Real Estate & Land

Identifying and evaluating land and projects, acquiring land and advancing transactions in collaboration with leading companies in the industry.

Identifying Opportunities. Connecting Capabilities. Advancing Projects.

The company’s real estate activity focuses on identifying and evaluating land and projects with potential, reviewing their planning status and advancing suitable transactions, including acquisitions and collaboration with leading companies in the field.

Agricultural Land

Land for Construction

Identifying and acquiring land designated for, or with potential for, future construction, including low-rise and high-density residential development.

Urban Renewal

Identifying and evaluating urban renewal projects, including evacuation-and-reconstruction projects, and advancing them in collaboration with leading companies in the field.

05

Combination Transactions

Identifying and advancing combination transactions in collaboration with leading companies in the construction and real estate sectors.

06

Project Marketing

Marketing projects to different target audiences, including projects tailored to the Haredi community.
