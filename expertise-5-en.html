Photovoltaic Farms

Land identification, planning advancement, development of solar farms and energy storage solutions.

From Land to Renewable Energy Generation

Photovoltaic farms make it possible to use suitable areas for generating electricity from solar energy. The company works to identify land, evaluate its suitability, advance planning and rezoning, establish contractual arrangements and develop the project in collaboration with leading companies in the field.

Activity Stages

Land IdentificationFeasibility ReviewPlanning & RezoningAcquisition / LeaseFarm ConstructionEnergy Storage
01

Land Identification

Identifying areas of at least 100 dunams and reviewing the site characteristics and initial suitability for a solar farm.

02

Feasibility Review

Reviewing site conditions, planning constraints, accessibility and key conditions affecting project feasibility.

03

Planning and Rezoning

Advancing planning and land rezoning in accordance with the requirements applicable to the establishment of a photovoltaic farm.

04

Acquisition / Lease

Advancing collaboration for the acquisition or lease of areas suitable for the project.

05

Farm Construction

Executing the project in collaboration with leading companies, including infrastructure and structural systems, panels, inverters, electrical systems and grid connection.

06

Energy Storage

Advancing and integrating energy storage projects in collaboration with leading companies and according to the project’s characteristics.
