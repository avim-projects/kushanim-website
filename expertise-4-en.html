From Quarry to Full Commercial Activity

Our quarry activity in Judea & Samaria includes advancing licensing and registration for new quarries, evaluating the renewal of activity at existing quarries, acquisition and leasing, operation, and the sale of quarry products for a wide range of uses in construction, infrastructure and industry.
